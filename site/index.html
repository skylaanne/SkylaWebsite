05 · how I approach problems

The same loop, whether it's a website or a pager going off.

while (true) { understand → explore → build → refine }

  1. step 01

    Understand

    Start with the problem, the goals, the audience, and the constraints. Ask the questions nobody thought to ask yet.

  2. step 02

    Explore

    Identify the right tools, ideas and approaches. Sometimes the answer is a new framework. Sometimes it's a phone call.

  3. step 03

    Build

    Turn the plan into a thoughtful, practical solution. Organized enough that future-me (or your team) can read it.

  4. step 04

    Refine

    Test, improve, and pay attention to the details that matter. Then watch it in production, because that's where the truth is.
